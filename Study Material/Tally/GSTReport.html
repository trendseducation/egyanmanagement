1. GST Reports Overview

GST returns are the periodic statements you file with the government, reporting your sales, purchases, and tax liability. Every registered business must file them — late filing attracts interest at 18% p.a. plus a late fee per day .

TallyPrime generates all major GST reports from the data you've already entered — no separate data entry. What you record in sales and purchase vouchers flows into GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and GSTR-9 automatically .

Understanding which return covers what is essential. GSTR-1 reports outward supplies (sales). GSTR-3B summarizes tax liability and ITC. GSTR-9 is the annual consolidation. Filing them in the right order — and reconciling with GSTR-2B — keeps you compliant.

Which returns apply to you? Regular taxpayers file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B monthly or quarterly (QRMP). Composition taxpayers file GSTR-4 quarterly and GSTR-9A annually. Small businesses may file GSTR-9 only if turnover exceeds ₹2 crore.
The GST return chain. Sales flow to GSTR-1. Purchases flow to GSTR-2B. Both combine into GSTR-3B. Everything consolidates into GSTR-9 annually.

2. GSTR-1 — Outward Supplies

GSTR-1 is the statement of outward supplies — every sale, credit note, and debit note you issued during the period. It's the primary return buyers use to claim their ITC, so accuracy is critical .

GSTR-1 is filed monthly or quarterly depending on your turnover. Businesses up to ₹5 crore can opt for the QRMP scheme (quarterly returns, monthly payment). Filing deadline: 11th of the following month for monthly filers, 13th for quarterly .

B2B Invoices (Table 4)

Every sale to a GST-registered buyer, with invoice number, GSTIN, taxable value, and tax. This is what populates your buyer's GSTR-2B.

B2C Sales (Table 7)

Sales to consumers (unregistered). Reported in summary — no individual invoice detail needed unless inter-state and above ₹2.5 lakh.

Credit / Debit Notes (Table 9B)

Sales returns, discounts, and invoice corrections. Must reference the original invoice. These reduce your Output GST liability.

Exports (Table 6A)

Zero-rated supplies with shipping bill details. Reported separately with port code and export type (with/without payment).

3. GSTR-3B — Summary Return

GSTR-3B is the summary return where you actually pay your tax. It consolidates your outward supplies (from GSTR-1) and your ITC (from GSTR-2B), showing the net tax liability for the period .

GSTR-3B must be filed monthly for all regular taxpayers, even those filing GSTR-1 quarterly under QRMP. The deadline is the 20th of the following month (or 22nd/24th for QRMP filers based on state category) .

Table 3.1 — Outward Supplies

Total taxable sales (excluding exempt, exports, and nil-rated). Includes amendments. Broken down by tax rate (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%, 3%).

Table 4 — Eligible ITC

Your Input Tax Credit from GSTR-2B, split into CGST, SGST, IGST, and Cess. This reduces your net liability. Must match IMS-accepted invoices.

Table 5 — Net Payable

Output Tax − Eligible ITC = Net Tax Payable. If ITC exceeds Output, the excess becomes a credit for next period — no cash payment needed.

ITC Reversal

If you used a wrong invoice, returned goods, or didn't pay a supplier within 180 days, ITC must be reversed. Reported in Table 4(B).

Order matters: File GSTR-1 first, then GSTR-3B. Buyers need your GSTR-1 to appear in their GSTR-2B before they can claim ITC. Filing in reverse order causes mismatches and disputes.
Late fee alert: GSTR-3B late fee is ₹50 per day (₹20 for nil returns), capped at ₹2,000 (₹500 for nil). Plus 18% annual interest on unpaid tax. A missed month can quickly become thousands in penalties .

4. GSTR-9 — Annual Return

GSTR-9 is the annual consolidation of all monthly/quarterly returns filed during the financial year. It reconciles what you reported month-by-month against your audited books .

GSTR-9 is mandatory for businesses with aggregate turnover above ₹2 crore. Below that threshold, filing is optional. The deadline is 31st December of the following financial year (e.g., GSTR-9 for FY 2025-26 must be filed by 31st December 2026) .

Part II — Outward Supplies

Annual totals of taxable sales, exports, exempt sales, and credit notes. Split by tax rate. Must match the sum of all GSTR-1 filings.

Part III — ITC

Annual ITC claimed, reversed, and ineligible. Must match GSTR-2B annual totals. Any gap needs an explanation in the reconciliation section.

Part IV — Tax Paid

Annual tax paid via cash and credit. Compares GSTR-3B totals with GSTR-9 declared amounts. Differences must be explained.

Part V — Reconciliation

Compares GSTR-9 declarations against your audited P&L and balance sheet. This is where auditors look for gaps between reported and actual transactions.

GSTR-9C (Reconciliation Statement): Required for businesses with turnover above ₹5 crore. It's a self-certified reconciliation of GSTR-9 with audited financial statements. Filed alongside GSTR-9 by 31st December .
Start early: GSTR-9 requires comparing 12 months of data. Don't start in December. Reconcile monthly, so that by year-end your GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and books already match. TallyPrime's annual reports help — generate the year-end summary as you go .

7. Tips & Best Practices

These practices prevent late fees, mismatches, and GST notices:

File GSTR-1 First

File GSTR-1 by the 11th (monthly) or 13th (quarterly) so buyers can claim ITC. Then file GSTR-3B by the 20th. Reverse order causes disputes.

Fix Uncertain Transactions

Before filing GSTR-1, check Uncertain Transactions. Missing GSTIN, blank HSN, and wrong rates will cause rejection on the portal. Fix in Tally first.

Reconcile GSTR-2B with IMS

Before GSTR-3B, verify your ITC matches the IMS-accepted invoices in GSTR-2B. Mismatches mean claiming ineligible credit — a notice risk.

Don't Wait for Deadline

Portals slow down on the 11th, 20th, and 31st. File by the 8th–10th to avoid last-minute server errors. Keep DSC charged and EVC mobile handy.

Save the Filed JSON

Download and save the JSON of every filed return. Also save the ARN (Acknowledgement Reference Number). These are your legal proof of filing.

Check ITC Reversal Triggers

Purchase returns, non-payment within 180 days, and credit notes trigger ITC reversal. Report these in GSTR-3B Table 4(B) — don't let them accumulate.

Amend Before Deadline

GSTR-1 amendments must be filed before the annual return or September of the next FY. Late amendments are not allowed. Track corrections monthly.

Never File with Wrong Data

Don't file to avoid late fees if data is wrong. A wrong return is worse than a late one — it triggers notices. Reconcile first, file correctly, pay late fee if needed.

Critical check: Before filing GSTR-3B, compare Output Tax in GSTR-3B with Output Tax in GSTR-1, and ITC in GSTR-3B with GSTR-2B accepted invoices. If they don't match, fix before filing. Post-filing corrections require amendments that can take months to resolve.
